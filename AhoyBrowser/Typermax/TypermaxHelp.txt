*** TYPERMAX INTRODUCTION ***

Typermax is a platform for training your fingers on the keyboard. Mastering the technique of touch typing (the 10-finger system) is crucial for obtaining a great score in Typermax.

Rule #1: Rest your index fingers on the F and J keys.
Rule #2: Always return to the home-row after each strike.
Rule #3: Don't look at the keyboard.


*** TYPERMAX GAMEPLAY RULES ***

Type the phrase displayed in the target display and keep the progress bar from reaching zero. Completing a phrase successfully will push the progress bar forward and buy you some extra time (the longer the phrase the more time you receive). 1 credit will be subtracted from your total credits when the progress bar reaches zero. At 0 credits it's game over.

Completing 1 phrase will gain you 10 points + the total word/numerics count times the current level, plus a level percentage bonus between 1 and 100. The more phrases you complete per level the higher the score. Completing a level will gain you a level bonus of 1000 points. Phrase difficulty is gradually increased along with the retraction speed of the timeout bar during level completion.

Striking the wrong key will impose a time penalty of -10% with each subsequent error (that follows immediately after) resulting in additional -2% per failed keystroke.

The phrase length cycle follows a fixed pattern containing words and numbers generated by random, using a built-in English dictionary and number chart. No word, number or phrase will appear twice in one game, unless you reach an ultra high level. In other words: every player will be served the same phrase length pattern but with different words and numbers.

Time bonus challenges are given at intervals (each time 30 phrases have been completed), consisting of long phrases representing useful information about typewriter history etc. Time bonus challenges will not add to level completion and only represent minor scores, as their primary purpose is to supply a time bonus.

High-scores are saved with encryption and can't be edited. You may reset your high-score by command input, using the command "tools.typermax.reset" via the command input dialog (called from the main window, PLOP and Utility editor using CTRL+ALT+A).


*** TRAINING MODE ***

Enable training mode to practice your typing skills. High-score is disabled during training mode.


*** TOUCH TYPING ***

Touch typing is a keyboard-typing method where the typist use all 10 fingers at once, without looking at the keyboard (also known as the 10-finger system). Each finger is assigned to a set of keys. The user practice this constellation until it becomes completely natural/automatic. This method will more than double the typing speed, compared to the typical "hunt and peck" typist.

Hunt and peck: 30 Words per minute (WPM)
Average beginner: 40 WPM
Professional: 80-100+ WPM
World record: 215 WPM in 1 minute on IBM Electric in 1946
