Time bonus challenge: The year 1910 is shown on Admiralty Arch, London, as MDCCCCX rather than MCMX. The numeral "I" can be placed before V and X to make 4 units (IV) and 9 units (IX) respectively. X can be placed before L and C to make 40 (XL) and 90 (XC) respectively. C can be placed before D and M to make 400 (CD) and 900 (CM) according to the same pattern. An example using the these rules would be 1904: this is composed of 1 (one thousand), 9 (nine hundreds), 0 (zero tens), and 4 (four units).
